It is one of the finest countries in Italy, has in general a temperate and healthy climate, and very few uncultivated spots, except the mountains, covered for a great part of the year with snow, the Maremma, and the frequent inundation of the rivers.Corn (of which it yields annually 1,500,000 quarters) flax, hemp, wine, fine fruits, vegetables, saffron, manna, linseed oil (upwards of 129,335 tons annually) chesnuts (above 783,000 bushels per annum) cork and timber. The natives of Tuscany are well made, industrious, sensible, affable, hospitable to strangers, entirely devoted to trade, and are all fond of what is termed pleasure.Or, six balls gules, the three uppermost of which are charged with fleurs de lys or, for Lorrain: surmounted with a crown and a fleur de lys or, or by a crowned crest, with a falcon argent.The order of knighthood of St. Stephen, instituted by Cosmus I. in 1561, is distinguished by a golden cross, pendent from a flame coloured ribbon. The knights wear on their breasts a crimson cross, set in gold. The grand duke is always grand master of the order.
The people are industrious, scheming, crafty, ignorant, wholly employed in trade, civil to strangers, and addicted to gaming.Argent, a cross gules, surmounted with a crown for the Island of Corsica.None.
